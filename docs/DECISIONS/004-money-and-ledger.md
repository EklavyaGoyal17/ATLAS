# ADR 004: Bounded minor units and paired journal postings
Context: floating point and one-sided ledger writes threaten accounting invariants. Decision: integer minor units with explicit currency, and indivisible debit/credit posting rows exposed through a ledger-entry view. Alternatives: arbitrary decimals; independently inserted entry rows with deferred balancing triggers. Tradeoff: simpler two-account transfers over a general multi-line journal. Consequences: captures/refunds fit the model, but fees, FX and split settlements require a new design. Reconciliation checks business totals as well as equal debits/credits. Review: before settlement/fees.
